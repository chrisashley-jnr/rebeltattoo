# Rebel Tattoos visual system

The opening route is a cinematic entrance. A dark, close photograph of tattoo equipment fills the viewport. The supplied Rebel mark and word logo sit at the upper center, followed by large stacked booking and home links. The home-service statement and Instagram handle anchor the lower center. Text and controls remain HTML over a text-free image.

The interior site uses near-black charcoal surfaces, ivory text, muted rose and oxblood accents, and warm pewter highlights. Barlow Condensed remains the display and control font; DM Sans remains the body font. Filled surfaces, restrained rounded corners, and visible focus rings remain consistent across public pages and the booking dashboard. Decorative borders are hidden.

The entrance is the `/` route. The full site begins at `/home`; booking remains directly available at `/booking`. The entrance adapts to narrow and short screens without covering the links or service note.
